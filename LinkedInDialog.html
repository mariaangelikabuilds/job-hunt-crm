Generate resume from LinkedIn

LinkedIn blocks bot fetches, so we work around it. Pick whichever path is easiest. Output: a Google Doc you can review, edit, and set as your master resume.
Tip: in your browser, hit Ctrl+A on the profile page, then Ctrl+C, then paste. Some browsers need you to scroll to lazy-load the full Experience section first.
On your own LinkedIn profile, click the "More" button under your headline, then "Save to PDF". Upload the PDF to Drive (or save it from a browser print-to-PDF). Open the file in Drive, copy its URL, paste here.
Take 1-6 screenshots of your own LinkedIn profile (logged in). Upload them to Drive. Paste the file URLs here, one per line. Claude vision reads them and builds the resume.
